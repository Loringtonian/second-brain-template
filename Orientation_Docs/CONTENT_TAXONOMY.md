## IDEA CATEGORIES (Taxonomy for Classification)

> Each category below carries a **Conceptual Territory** (what belongs here) and **Recognition keywords** (how to spot it). These are the reusable parts. Replace the per-category "examples" with your own as the brain fills (`__FILL_FROM_USER__:category_examples`).

### Inventions/Startups - Bits (Software)
**What it is:** Software and digital business ideas

**Conceptual Territory:** Any software product or service idea — apps, platforms, websites, SaaS, AI/LLM-powered tools and wrappers, digital automation/productivity tools, online services or marketplaces, browser extensions, plugins, bots; anything that runs on computers/phones without a physical component.

**Recognition keywords:** "app", "platform", "software", "SaaS", "digital", "automation", "tool", "LLM wrapper", "plugin", "bot", "website", "service"

---

### Inventions/Startups - Atoms (Physical/Hardware)
**What it is:** Physical products, hardware, manufacturing, robotics

**Conceptual Territory:** Any physical product idea — hardware, machines, devices, gadgets, wearables, fitness equipment, furniture, robotics, drones, manufacturing/construction, food/beverage products; anything you can hold, touch, or physically interact with.

**Recognition keywords:** "robot", "hardware", "physical", "manufacturing", "build", "machine", "kit", "device", "wearable", "product"

### Journal - Intellectual Writings
**What it is:** Philosophical observations, AI commentary, technical analysis, frameworks

**Conceptual Territory:** Philosophical observations and frameworks; analysis of technology, AI, society; commentary on current events in tech; predictions and forecasting; essays exploring ideas at length; outward-facing observations about the world that aren't personal diary entries.

**Recognition keywords:** "observation", "analysis", "insight", "framework", "theory", "prediction"

**How to distinguish:** Journal-Intellectual is outward-facing (analyzing the world). Personal Journal is inward-facing (analyzing the self).

---

### Personal Journal
**What it is:** Personal reflections, life updates, self-improvement, introspection

**Conceptual Territory:** Personal reflections and introspection; life updates and daily experiences; emotional processing; self-improvement plans; relationship thoughts; health/wellness reflections; personal planning and life direction.

**Recognition keywords:** "I feel", "today", "personal", "life", "habit", "my experience"

**How to distinguish:** Look for entries where the subject is the self, not the external world. Analyzing AI → Intellectual. Analyzing one's own reaction to AI → Personal.

---

### Predictions
**What it is:** Forecasts with explicit timeline thinking

**Conceptual Territory:** Explicit predictions about the future; timeline-based forecasts (by year X, in N years); bets or probability estimates; technology roadmap predictions; societal-change forecasts.

**Recognition keywords:** "predict", "will happen", "timeline", "by [year]", "forecast", "in 5 years"

**How to distinguish:** Must have an explicit forward-looking claim with a timeline or probability. General musings about the future without specific claims belong in Journal-Intellectual.

## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

When parsing a new source file:

1. **Check if an authoritative source exists** - Before mining, check if this topic already has developed content in the relevant folder.
2. **Identify project references** - Does it mention one of the owner's projects? Map to the correct bucket.
3. **Spot theme patterns** - Recurring themes get tagged appropriately.
4. **Recognize people** - Map recurring names to topics (`__FILL_FROM_USER__:key_people` — e.g. a given commentator → AI commentary; a given economist → economics).
5. **Apply frameworks** - The owner's recurring mental frameworks help route ambiguous content.
6. **Check for TO-DOs** - Action items get extracted to TODO_MASTER.md.
7. **Preserve exact language** - The owner's keywords are sacred for memory retrieval. Don't paraphrase.
8. **Cross-reference** - Link to existing authoritative sources when they exist.
