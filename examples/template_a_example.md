## Original Text
Okay so I'm standing at the tide line and there's this whole world in the pools that appears for like two hours twice a day and then it's gone. And I'm thinking — the data nobody is collecting is the data sitting in ten thousand pockets. Everyone takes photos of this stuff. What if the photo was the logging action? You open the app at low tide, point it at a pool, it IDs the species, stamps the GPS and the tide height, and now you're a sensor. Do that across a whole coastline and you've got a biodiversity map that no research budget could ever fund. The walk was going to happen anyway. You're just not throwing away the signal.
